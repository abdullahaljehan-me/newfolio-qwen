Hi, I'm Jehan — an ambitious science student from Dhaka with a rock-solid foundation in mathematics and physics, currently gearing up for a career in engineering. I'm passionate about bridging the gap between theory and practice, with a deep dive into Embedded Systems, IoT, and Programming.

My approach is highly analytical and grounded in a "learn by doing" philosophy — building, breaking, and iterating until the system is robust. I'm proud to be a Founding Advisor at Kynatium Labs, where we're making technology fundamentals and embedded systems education accessible to students across Bangladesh.

Long-term: contribute to cutting-edge research in smart systems & robotics, pursue graduate studies abroad, and engineer at top-tier tech innovators.
